# Superstore-Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset to identify sales trends, profitability drivers, customer performance, product performance, and the impact of discounts on business profitability.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to provide actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.


## Dataset Information

- Dataset: Sample Superstore
- Total Orders: 9,994
- Total Customers: 794
- Total Sales: $2.28M
- Total Profit: $286.40K
- Total Quantity Sold: 37,873


## Tools Used

- Google Sheets
  - Data Validation
  - Data Cleaning
  - Exploratory Data Analysis

- Power BI
  - Data Modeling
  - DAX Measures
  - Interactive Dashboard Development
  - Business Intelligence Reporting


## Data Cleaning Process

The following checks were performed:

- Checked for missing values
- Verified duplicate Order IDs
- Validated date fields
- Analyzed negative shipping days
- Reviewed sales, profit, and discount values
- Standardized dataset structure for reporting

# Key Insights

## Sales Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales.
- The West region contributed the highest revenue.
- Sales showed consistent growth over the analyzed period.

## Profit Insights

- Technology was the most profitable category.
- Furniture generated the lowest profit among all categories.
- Several products generated significant losses despite strong sales.

## Product Insights

### Top Selling Product

- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier

### Top Loss-Making Product

- Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print

### High Revenue Sub-Categories

- Copiers
- Phones
- Chairs

## Customer Insights

Top profit-generating customers include:

- Tamara Chand
- Raymond Buch
- Sanjit Chand

## Discount Analysis

- Higher discount levels generally reduced profitability.
- Many highly discounted orders resulted in losses.
- A negative relationship exists between discount and profit.


# Business Recommendations

### 1. Optimize Discount Strategy

Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products.

### 2. Focus on High-Profit Products

Increase marketing efforts for profitable Technology products.

### 3. Review Loss-Making Products

Evaluate pricing and discount policies for products generating consistent losses.

### 4. Retain High-Value Customers

Develop retention strategies for top-performing customers.

### 5. Expand Successful Regional Strategies

Apply successful sales approaches from high-performing regions to weaker regions.
